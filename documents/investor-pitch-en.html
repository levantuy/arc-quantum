Investment Thesis

DeFi only scales when the experience stops feeling expert-only.

Most DeFi products still require users to navigate fragmented tools while managing chains, tokens, fees, and transaction states on their own. Arc Quantum addresses that friction directly by turning the highest-value actions into one coherent product experience.

Current Status

The MVP has a clear direction and a technical foundation deep enough for commercialization.

The product already has a defined architecture with a Next.js frontend, backend APIs, PostgreSQL via Prisma, wallet authentication, configuration management, and operational logging.

Investor Angle

The opportunity sits in the experience layer and the operations layer, not only in smart contracts.

As Arc expands its application base and asset flows, demand should increase for a trusted entry point covering bridging, swapping, balance management, and token transfers. Arc Quantum is positioning itself at that interaction layer.

Problem

Market problem

DeFi adoption is constrained less by product availability than by complexity, fragmentation, and the cost of user error.

01

Too many steps, too many contexts

Bridging, swapping, checking balances, and sending assets are often fragmented across multiple applications. That creates serious friction for non-expert users.

02

Poor visibility into transaction progress

Many interfaces only show the final result. Users struggle to understand the current state, the cause of a failure, or what to do next when something breaks.

03

A gap between UX and infrastructure

Many teams invest heavily in on-chain infrastructure while underinvesting in the product layer that makes that infrastructure usable at scale.

Solution

How Arc Quantum solves it

An Arc-native DeFi product that consolidates essential asset workflows into one clear, consistent, and operationally reliable experience.

“From fragmented crypto operations to one coherent Arc-native operating surface.”

Direct wallet connectivity, no private key custody Responsive support across desktop and mobile Designed to expand into new chains and modules

Bridge

Move assets across chains on Arc with route configuration checks, input validation, logging, and transaction-state visibility.

Swap

Swap tokens on Arc Network through a simple interface built for mainstream users rather than trader-centric complexity.

Unified Balance

Aggregate asset balances into a clearer portfolio view so users do not need to inspect each token or touchpoint separately.

Send

Transfer tokens to another address with a clearer UX for payments, transfers, and everyday activity on Arc.
